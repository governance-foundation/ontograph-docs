Personnel and research appointments

Recorded and independently reproduced on Windows, 8 October 2026. This demonstration follows one person across personnel and research staffing views, using explicit Appointment relators and mediation. It shows how to inspect a recorded appointment and its evidence before treating it as valid staffing. The demonstration was integrated into the source repository; this page presents retained execution evidence rather than a new installed run.

The organisational question​

Personnel identifies Alex as person:a. Research records two Appointment relators, appointment:one and appointment:two, each mediating that person and organization:one, the Research Institute. Another person, person:b, also has the label Alex and participates in the seminar, but has no declared research appointment. Matching labels do not merge their source identities.

At 10:30Z, two time-qualified membership records are active for person:a. At 12:00Z, the first has ended; the second remains active until 13:00Z. The appointment SELECT still returns both recorded appointment links: it is not time-filtered. The active-record answer comes from the importer's queryRecords, retained inside each composition report. Two appointments describe two grounding records for one person, rather than two people or an entitlement decision.

When an appointment's organisation participant is missing, its recorded membership can remain present while its grounding fails finite-graph validation. A count of recorded roles alone does not establish valid staffing or authority.

What actually ran​

Model JSON and separately bound instance evidence passed through the actual source importer. Product commands generated RDF and SHACL, ran the pinned OWL provider and Apache Jena, and persisted and reloaded the resulting composition. Selected SPARQL answers were checked against frozen expectations before and after reload, followed by source-assisted reimport. Selected CLI/API parity also passed. The target graphs were generated from source.

Five scenarios ran twice with equivalent normalized semantic results. Each complete independent reproduction executed 168 operations: 153 CLI and 15 HTTP, including 40 OWL CLI and 79 Jena CLI operations. These subsets are included in the total. The evidence index records candidate identities, reproduction receipts and download hashes. Website builds check retained bytes; they do not repeat the installed journey.

Under the open-world assumption, OWL can allow unnamed witnesses for missing participants. SHACL checks the finite recorded graph and reports the missing participant. OWL consistency therefore does not establish SHACL conformance. Explicitly sourced mediation is entailed; timeless Researcher membership is not entailed. Equal decimal quality values do not entail equality of their quality individuals. Separate identifiers do not assert OWL inequality; no unique-name assumption is made. Non-entailment is not explicit negation.

How the nine recommended gUFO examples fit​

All nine examples from the pinned gUFO revision are separately accounted for: endurant types, objects and parts, qualities, relators, events, situations, higher-order types, invalid endurant types and invalid higher-order types. Fresh installed Jena reference-graph checks report seven conformant and two nonconformant, matching recorded expectations. In this original organisational-demo snapshot, for all nine, complete-example generation was unsupported and not executed. Nine checked graphs do not mean nine generated transformations. A subsequent nine-example delivery now demonstrates direct Turtle admission and regeneration of every source assertion, including independent full-suite reproduction. That successor remains distinct from general OntoUML JSON-to-gUFO transformation.

The September gUFO review retains the historical Pellet/pySHACL baseline and source/shape evidence separately. It is not a fresh October OWL run of those examples. gUFO and gufoshapes are attributed to the NEMO contributors under CC BY 4.0; see the linked upstream source for author credits and retain its copyright notices when reusing material.

What remains to establish​

Comparison demonstrates source-assisted byte reproduction, RDF graph isomorphism after reload and selected exact typed query answers. It does not establish a general target-only inverse or complete semantic equivalence across formats. Recorded capability gaps include full modal rigidity/identity, complete normative interactions and unsupported source families/qualified association cardinalities.

The journey does not yet demonstrate a staffing UI, entitlement decisions or appointment rights and duties. Execution here is Windows-only; no second backend, held-out assurance, expert endorsement, product release or deployment is asserted. At the demonstration checkpoint, comprehensive C0–C7 remained 0/8. These artifacts contribute bounded C2/C4/C5/C6 evidence without closing that programme. The two read-model reference cases retain their separate case and question counts.

For semantic feedback: does recorded time-qualified appointment grounding adequately express this organisational case, and what further source evidence would interpret the appointments as normative rights and duties?
