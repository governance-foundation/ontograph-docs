02 / Worked conceptual case

A person is more than
their appointment.

Personnel and research staffing describe the same people through different concerns. The source explicitly distinguishes a Person, a Researcher role and the Appointment that grounds participation in an organisation.

Recorded case · independently reproduced Reviewed source candidate with actual operations; canonical adoption is not established.
Read the complete diagram text alternative

Person and Organization are explicitly declared Kinds. Researcher is explicitly declared a Role specializing Person. Appointment is a Relator with two separately identified mediation relations: one to Person and one to Organization. Each appointment has 1..* Person participants and 1..* Organization participants. Each person and organisation may participate in 0..* appointments. These are source commitments, not a claim that generated generic SHACL enforces each typed minimum.

Identity

Two people, one label

person:a and person:b share a display label. That does not merge their source identities or assert OWL inequality. Only the first has declared research membership.

Dependence

Two grounding records

Two overlapping appointments connect person:a with organization:one. An appointment is represented explicitly, rather than flattened into an unexplained edge.

Time

Membership is qualified

The actual query returns two active membership records at 10:30Z and one at noon. This comes from explicit membership intervals; an end date alone does not establish a role transition.

A validation gap worth examining

Generic generated SHACL checks a total minimum of two mediation values. It does not independently enforce one class-correct participant for each source relation. A generic pass therefore cannot establish the source’s complete grounding commitment.

04 / Interpreting results

A consistent graph can still
describe an invalid model.

Keep the questions separate. A passing tool result has meaning only in relation to its inputs, assumptions and expected conclusion.

01

Consistency

Do the selected OWL axioms admit an interpretation? Missing recorded participants may still have unnamed witnesses under an open-world interpretation.

02

Entailment

Does every interpretation satisfy the supplied conclusion? A conclusion that is not entailed has not thereby been disproved.

03

Validation

Does this finite recorded graph satisfy these SHACL shapes? Diagnostic signatures point to specific facts and constraints.

04

Query answers

Which bindings match the stated query? A timeless appointment-link query does not answer a time-qualified staffing question.

Conceptual correctness needs independent modelling judgement and justified commitments in addition to tool outcomes.
