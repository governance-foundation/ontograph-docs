Source identity ≠ acceptance

Distinct source identifiers remain connected across views. Null canonical references mean no accepted canonical object is demonstrated. Equal labels and values imply neither identity nor inequality.

A projection has a purpose

OWL/gUFO presents selected meaning. Explicit temporal facts also use composition vocabulary. Modal identity, anti-rigidity, rights and duties are not proved by a class label or a diagram.

Validation is specific

The two mediation relations each declare a minimum of one participant. Generic SHACL’s minimum of two total gufo:mediates values does not independently enforce those typed minima.
